Tools

Useful tools with safe commands.

Subfinder

Passive subdomain discovery for authorized targets and labs.

subfinder -d example.com -all

httpx

Check live hosts, status codes, and web titles.

httpx -l targets.txt -status-code -title

Nuclei

Template-based scanner for legal and authorized testing only.

nuclei -l targets.txt
